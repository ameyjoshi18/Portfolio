01Core Banking Migration

FIS CBS → Finacle CBS

Problem
A live bank's core system had to change underneath it — there's no maintenance window big enough to take a bank offline.
Role
UAT governance, business sign-off, cross-team coordination
What I did
Sequenced the migration from FIS to Finacle so the bank could replace the engine while it kept running, and closed out business sign-off before go-live.
Systems
FISFinacleTCS BaNCSUAT

Outcome — a core banking migration executed on a live bank, without anyone outside noticing.

02Financial Access Rails

AePS Integration

Problem
Aadhaar-based authentication has to succeed through UIDAI, an acquirer-to-NPCI-to-issuer hop, and still degrade gracefully when the biometric read fails.
Role
Requirement analysis, BRD/FRD, UAT coordination
What I did
Defined the authentication and settlement flow across acquirer, NPCI and issuer, and specified what happens operationally when a biometric read fails.
Systems
UIDAINPCIAcquirerIssuer

Outcome — a validated AePS journey with clear failure handling instead of a silent drop.

03Onboarding + Money Movement

Domestic Money Transfer

Problem
Agent-assisted cash-in has to move money correctly the first time, across an IMPS leg and NPCI switching layer neither side can fully see.
Role
Business analysis, requirement definition, UAT coordination
What I did
Mapped the agent cash-in flow, the IMPS leg that actually moves the money, NPCI switching in between, and reconciliation after.
Systems
Agent appIMPSNPCI switchReconciliation

Outcome — a transfer flow where the numbers match at the end of the day, and failures trace back to a specific hop.

04Card Security

Cards & EMV

Problem
Contactless, NCMC and EMV 3DS all touch the same PIN and card data — one weak link is enough.
Role
Business analysis, security requirement validation
What I did
Specified how contactless and NCMC transit cards authenticate, how EMV 3DS handles online authentication, and how PIN data stays encrypted the whole way through.
Systems
EMV 3DSNCMCPIN encryptionIssuer

Outcome — card authentication paths that hold up under audit, not just under a demo.
